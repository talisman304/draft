Patients

Practical information for new patients and returning patients.

New patients

  1. Book: Choose a consultation time via phone or form.
  2. Assessment: We review concerns, history, and priorities.
  3. Plan: Clear options, benefits/risks, and next steps.
What should I expect at my first visit?

A calm, structured consultation. We’ll discuss your goals, examine oral health, and recommend options. You’ll leave with a clear plan and cost estimates.

Do you offer nervous-patient support?

Yes. We can plan for comfort, explain each step, and go at your pace. Please mention any anxiety when booking so we can accommodate.

Returning patients

  • Routine check-ups and hygiene scheduling
  • Review of treatment progress and maintenance
  • Retainers, whitening top-ups, and repairs
How often should I come in?

Most patients benefit from check-ups every 6–12 months. Hygiene frequency varies by risk factors and gum health—your clinician will advise.
